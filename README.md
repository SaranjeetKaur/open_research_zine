# About this project

The Zine titled "Open research good practices: A wildlife perspective" is inspired by the wildlife around the Centre of Alternative Technology, Machynlleth, Wales. It was created during the [UK Reproducibility Network](https://www.ukrn.org) hackathon at the Open Science Retreat Global Edition 2026. The wildlife featured in the zine include bees, badgers, hedgehogs, tawny owls, and wild foxes. It includes four categories of open research practices and resources, namely, open outputs, open data, open methods, and open education, based on the document on "[Open research across disciplines](https://docs.google.com/document/d/1oFFHUmKPfTbDf5O8EvOcZvSSdMLMDO-r/edit)".
